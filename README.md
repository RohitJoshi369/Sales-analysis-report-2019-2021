**Sales Analysis Dashboard (2019–2021)**
This project is a sales dashboard I built to explore and understand business performance over a three-year period (2019–2021). The goal was to turn raw sales data into something visual and easy to interpret, highlighting trends, patterns, and key areas of performance.

**What this dashboard shows**
The dashboard gives a quick snapshot of overall performance along with a deeper breakdown:
Total sales, profit, quantity, and discounts at a glance
Sales split by customer segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
Regional performance across the US
Sales trends over time (by order date)
State-level distribution on a map
Product performance by sub-category

**A few things I noticed while building it**
The Consumer segment clearly drives most of the revenue
The West region consistently performs better than others
Categories like Phones and Chairs stand out as top contributors
Sales aren’t completely steady — there are noticeable spikes at certain times of the year

**Tools used**

Standard visualization techniques for dashboards
